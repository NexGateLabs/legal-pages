Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2025

Thank you for using My Immigration Helper (the "App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address, password, and optional display name.
  • Third-Party Login: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your basic profile information (such as name and email).
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g., device type, app performance) to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App’s features.
  • To authenticate and secure your account.
  • To send important updates such as account verification emails.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (database, authentication, storage) and Expo SecureStore (on-device storage). We implement industry-standard security measures, but no system is 100% secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your data. We may share limited data with third-party providers (e.g., Google or Apple for authentication) solely to enable login functionality.

5. Your Rights

  • You may access, update, or delete your account at any time within the App.
  • You may request deletion of your account by contacting us at support@myimmigrationhelper.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the App and on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@myimmigrationhelper.com.
